Electro Store Knowledge Base

About Electro Store

Electro Store is an online electronics store
specializing in laptops, mobile phones,
monitors and other electronic products.

Customers can ask Electro Store support for help
choosing products or answering questions about
orders, returns and warranties.

Return Policy

Customers can return eligible products within
14 days of receiving the product.

The product should be in good condition and
should include the original accessories.

Contact customer support before returning a product
to confirm its eligibility and the return steps.
The return policy does not specify refund timing,
refund methods or a complete list of exclusions.
Ask customer support to confirm these details.

Laptop Warranty

All laptops purchased from Electro Store have
a 12 month warranty. Contact customer support for
help with a warranty question or claim. Specific
coverage, exclusions and claim requirements are
not listed here and should be confirmed with support.

Mobile Phone Warranty

Mobile phones purchased from Electro Store have
a 12 month warranty. Contact customer support for
help with a warranty question or claim. Specific
coverage, exclusions and claim requirements are
not listed here and should be confirmed with support.

Monitor Warranty

Monitors purchased from Electro Store have
a 24 month warranty. Contact customer support for
help with a warranty question or claim. Specific
coverage, exclusions and claim requirements are
not listed here and should be confirmed with support.

Shipping

Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 5
business days.

Express delivery normally takes 1 to 2
business days.

These are normal delivery estimates, not guaranteed
delivery dates. Shipping prices, delivery areas and
order-specific delivery dates are not specified here.
Check the order details or ask customer support for
information about a particular shipment.

Customer Support

Customer support is available Monday to Friday
from 9 AM to 5 PM.

Customers can contact support for questions
about orders, returns, warranties and products.
The support contact method and time zone are not
specified in this knowledge base.
